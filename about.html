LensRift is an ongoing exploration of cinematic observation, treating ordinary settings as dynamic visual canvases.

Everything is captured on a Fujifilm X-M5, emphasizing natural lighting around Citraland and greater Surabaya. To ensure the narrative pacing feels intentional, all visual sequences are cut and graded in Premiere Pro, transforming raw footage into cohesive visual stories.
